## Answer

Pack averages ~3.4/5. Only **earnings-analysis** clears the clean bar (4.8). The other eight skills are usable SOP skeletons but thin on references (R=2), pack-wide citation/freshness, compliance, and cross-skill handoffs. **initiating-coverage** is content-rich (R=5) but dragged by trigger UX friction and internal contradictions (6 vs 15+ model tabs, font policy, file-tree extension). Systemic: quality bifurcation, modeling stack fork vs `financial-analysis`, missing ER cycle orchestration.
